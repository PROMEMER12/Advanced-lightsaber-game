# Advanced-lightsaber-game
a roblox game heavily focussed on lightsaber combat.


# What it includes

- over 10 lightsaber types (single, dual, staff)
- over 27 maps
- 13 + force powers
- over 80 costum designed models
- over 400 costum anims
- Never really marketed right (😢)
- costum npc
- costum spaceships
- costum blasters
- fairly optimized + good quality
- hilts give credits based on wich hilt they use


# Combat system

heavily based on stamina!

- stamina drains on blocks/swings
- stamina can be regained by parries
- if your stamina is under 30% then you can lose arms and legs or even your head
- if its above 30 you lose a bit of stamina on hit and lose health but most of the time you dont die!


many moves you can do!

- Block + r = frontstab
- Block + q = spinmove
- Block + r + air = palpatine move
- 3 attacks then jump = air spin
- q = punch
- q + air = kick
- r = throw
- r + air = ground stab
- c = switch modes between heavy/light attacks
- e = dash
- e + facing your back towards someone = overhead jump swing
